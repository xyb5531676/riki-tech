What we do

CBAP-Certified Business Analysis, Globally Delivered

Riki-Tech's Business Analysis service is delivered by IIBA-certified CBAP Business Analysts — all services follow IIBA BABOK skills and methodology. We improve product and project efficiency across the entire lifecycle, delivering solutions that bring sustainable business value and identify bottlenecks that could impact software efficiency.

Business analysts are a vital communication bridge between stakeholders and the development team — they speak both business and technology language. Our analysts work seamlessly within your agile teams, embedding themselves into your delivery pipeline.

Whether your team is co-located or distributed, our service can be delivered on-site at your premises or fully remote — making it practical and effective for organisations anywhere in the world.

AI-Powered Delivery

Business Analysis Accelerated
by BMAD & AI

Our Business Analysis service uniquely integrates with Riki-Tech's proprietary BMAD framework (Breakthrough Method for Agile AI-Driven Development) — enabling us to leverage the world's most capable AI large language models to dramatically accelerate the path from requirements to working code.

Instead of traditional, slow hand-offs between BA and development, BMAD allows our analysts to work directly with AI models such as Claude, ChatGPT, and Qwen to rapidly generate, validate, and iterate on user stories, specifications, and code — compressing delivery timelines without sacrificing rigour.
