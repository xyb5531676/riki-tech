TOGAF® Consulting

Enterprise Architecture Consulting Service

TOGAF® certified Enterprise Architects helping organisations design, implement and govern IT infrastructure truly aligned to business strategy — quickly and effectively.

What is TOGAF used for?

TOGAF® (The Open Group Architecture Framework) is used to help organisations design an IT infrastructure adapted to meet their requirements. Trained TOGAF® professionals communicate with department heads and help design and implement an IT strategy quickly and effectively.

Our TOGAF EA Consulting Service covers all four architecture domains — Business, Data, Application, and Technology — using the Architecture Development Method (ADM) as our core process.

TOGAF® certified Enterprise Architects at Riki-Tech provide EA consulting services — from initial maturity assessment through to full architecture repository delivery using ArchiMate modelling.

TOGAF Architecture Development Method (ADM)

© The Open Group

ADM Phases

P

Preliminary Phase

Preparation and initiation — TOGAF framework customisation and Architecture Principles definition.

A

Architecture Vision

Scope, stakeholders, Architecture Vision — obtain approval to proceed with development.

B

Business Architecture

Business Architecture development — processes, functions, roles and governance.

C

Information Systems

Data and application architectures supporting the agreed Architecture Vision.

D

Technology Architecture

IT infrastructure, middleware, networks, communications and processing standards.

E

Opportunities & Solutions

Implementation planning — work packages, projects, and transition architectures.

F

Migration Planning

Baseline to Target Architecture migration — detailed Implementation and Migration Plan.

G

Implementation Governance

Architectural oversight of the implementation — ensuring conformance throughout delivery.

H

Change Management

Procedures for managing change to the new architecture over time.

RM

Requirements Management

Managing architecture requirements throughout the ADM — the central hub connecting all phases.

Four Architecture Domains

🏢

Business Architecture

🗄️

Data Architecture

⚙️

Technology Architecture

Software and hardware capabilities required for business, data, and application services.
