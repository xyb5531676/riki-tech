What is BitHarvest?

BitHarvest is a next-generation Web3 platform that redefines Bitcoin mining through innovation, decentralisation, and sustainability. Founded in 2023 and headquartered in Kuala Lumpur, BitHarvest has rapidly become a global leader in AI-powered crypto mining technology.

BitHarvest merges a cutting-edge cryptocurrency booster mining platform with the dynamic capabilities of a fintech innovator — founded on the stability of advanced blockchain technology. Their flagship product, the BitBooster device, enables anyone to participate in Bitcoin mining without purchasing expensive ASIC hardware, managing electricity, or requiring technical knowledge.

BitHarvest is registered in the British Virgin Islands (BVI) under company number 2160035 and holds an MSB licence granted by the US regulator FinCEN — providing regulatory certainty for global investors and partners.
